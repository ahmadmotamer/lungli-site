HelloTalk vs Tandem — and free alternatives worth trying (2026)

By the Lungli team · Published 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

HelloTalk and Tandem are the two best-known language exchange apps, and both are free to start. Here is how they differ, and which other free options are worth a look when neither suits you.

HelloTalk and Tandem in short

HelloTalk feels like a social network for learners. Besides one-to-one chats with text and voice messages, it has a public feed where people post in the language they are learning and others correct them, and live voice rooms. It is free, with a paid VIP plan that removes ads and lifts the daily limits on tools such as translation.

Tandem focuses on one-to-one conversations. You browse or search for partners and talk by text, voice messages or calls. It is free, with a paid Pro plan that adds features such as more search filters.

Both mainly live on your phone, both have very large communities, and both put some of their tools behind a subscription. The plans change often, so check each app's own page for what is free today.

Which one fits you

  • You like to post and get corrected by many people at once — HelloTalk's feed suits you.
  • You prefer a few steady partners and real conversations — Tandem's one-to-one approach suits you.
  • You do not want a subscription at all, or you prefer a computer — look at the alternatives below.

Speaky is a simple, casual way to find people to chat with, and its main features cost nothing. Conversation Exchange is one of the oldest exchange sites, and still one of the few that helps you find a partner to meet face to face in your own city. The Mixxer, run by Dickinson College, is popular with schools and teachers; it introduces you, and you arrange the conversation yourselves. Reddit's r/language_exchange is a busy board of people looking for partners — no profile to build, but no matching or safety tools either.

Lungli, honestly

Lungli is a newer, free language exchange that works in your browser. What it does well:

  • Two-way matching: it suggests people who speak what you learn and learn what you speak, and shows why you match. Advanced search finds anyone by the language they speak or learn, in 80 languages.
  • Corrections in the chat: your partner corrects a message where it is, with the natural version and, if they like, a short note on why. You can save corrections and words to your Memory.
  • Groups for every level in 24 languages.
  • Safety: block, report, pause your profile, or let only people you have talked to write to you; your email address is never shown; people aged 16 and 17 only meet each other; no dating.

What it does not have: voice or video calls, built-in translation, and — being new — the size of HelloTalk's or Tandem's community. If you want to talk by video today, use it with a video app you already have.

How to choose

Try two at once for a week. Keep the one where people actually reply and where you learn something from every conversation — that matters more than any list of features.
