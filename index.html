What is a language exchange?

In a language exchange, two people help each other: you practise their language, they practise yours. It is real conversation with a real person, it costs nothing, and it fits around your day.

Questions

Is Lungli free?

Yes. Lungli is free to use, and it shows no ads.

Is it a dating app?

No. Lungli is for practising languages. Flirting with people who have not asked for it, sexual messages and asking for photos break the rules, and you can block or report anyone.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 16 or over. People aged 16 and 17 are only matched with each other.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Lungli works in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

Can other people see my email address?

Never. Messages go through Lungli, and your email address never leaves your account.

How do I find a language exchange partner?

Tell Lungli the languages you speak and the one you are learning. It suggests people who speak what you are learning and are learning what you speak, and shows why you match. Advanced search finds anyone by the language they speak or learn.

Which languages can I practise?

Any of 80 languages, from English, Spanish and Arabic to Swahili, Icelandic and Tagalog. There are groups in 24 of them.
